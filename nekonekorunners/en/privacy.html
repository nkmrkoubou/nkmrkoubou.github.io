Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

The developer of Nekoneko Runners (the "Developer") sets out how user information is handled in the iPhone app "Nekoneko Runners" (the "App") as follows.

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location through the App. The App has no account registration, and it does not send information to any server run by the Developer.

Your game progress (best record, fish collected, outfits, mission progress, sound and language settings, etc.) is saved only on your iPhone.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Ads are shown in the following situations:

  • A full-screen ad shown once every few game overs
  • A video ad shown only when you choose "Watch Video & Continue" yourself

Google AdMob may collect the following information to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and the approximate region estimated from it
  • Device information such as the device model and OS version
  • History of ad views and taps
  • App performance and crash information

This information is handled by Google, and the Developer does not receive it in a form that identifies individuals. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and page about advertising.

3. Tracking permission

To show more relevant ads, the App may ask for permission to track when you first launch it. You can use all features of the App even if you do not allow it (in that case, ads not based on your interests are shown).

You can change this permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Users in regions where consent is required by law, such as Europe, are shown an ad consent form. You can change your consent at any time from "Ad Privacy Settings" on the title screen.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because the Developer does not collect personal information, the Developer does not provide personal information to third parties.

5. Children

The App is not directed at any particular age group. The Developer does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Parents may also use iPhone features such as Screen Time as needed.

6. Changes to this policy

The Developer may change this policy as needed. Any changes will be announced on this page.
